# Key-Revenue-Drivers-by-Category-
## This project takes a closer look at Axis &amp; Co.’s 2024 sales to see which products, payment methods, and channels brought in the most revenue. It also explores how customer choices shaped overall performance. The goal is to help the company make smarter decisions for marketing, inventory, and future growth.


## E-Commerce Revenue Insights – Project Overview

This project focuses on understanding how different product categories, payment methods, and sales channels drive revenue for Axis & Co., an e-commerce business. The goal is to uncover which areas generated the most revenue in 2024 and how customer preferences shaped overall performance, helping the company make smarter decisions for marketing and inventory planning.

Established in 2020, Axis & Co. sells a wide range of products — from accessories and electronics to stationery, furniture, and apparel — through both online and in-store channels. As the market becomes more competitive and customer behavior continues to evolve, Axis & Co. is turning to data-driven insights to refine its sales strategy, understand its 2024 performance, and maintain steady, sustainable growth in the years ahead.

# Insights Summary

## Top-Performing Categories by Revenue

  •	Accessories led with $2.55 M (27.90%) in revenue, followed closely by Electronics at $2.44 M (26.73%).
  
  •	Stationery ($1.66 M, 18.20%) and Furniture ($1.64 M, 17.19%) contributed solidly, while Apparel ($845 K, 9.24%) ranked lowest.
  
  •	Accessories and Electronics together represented over 54% of total revenue, underscoring their central role in driving sales growth.


## Product Performance

•	The Wall Clock (Accessories) generated the highest single-product revenue at $929 K (10.16%), followed by Backpack ($828 K, 9.06%) and White Mug ($794 K, 8.69%).

•	Electronics items such as USB Cable ($792 K, 8.67%) and Wireless Mouse ($790 K, 8.65%) ranked next.

•	Stationery products like Notebook ($861 K, 9.43%) and Blue Pen ($804 K, 8.80%) performed above average within their category.

•	Furniture items such as Desk Lamp ($890 K, 9.75%) maintained steady performance, while Apparel (T-shirt) at $845 K (9.24%) lagged behind.

## Payment Methods

•	PayPal: $3.12 M

•	Bank Transfer: $3.03 M

•	Credit Card: $2.99 M

All three methods performed comparably, with PayPal showing a slight lead, suggesting customers’ preference for secure digital payments.


## Sales Channels

•	Online: $4.58 M

•	In-store: $4.57 M

Revenue distribution between channels was nearly even, highlighting effective omnichannel management. However, online channels slightly edged out in total sales, indicating digital growth potential.

## Month-over-Month Revenue Trends (2024)

The business experienced revenue volatility throughout the year:

•	Positive peaks occurred in March (+75 K, +10.69%), July (+36 K, +5.03%), and August (+38 K, +4.96%), driven by strong promotional campaigns.

•	Negative dips were seen in February (–29 K, –4.02%), April (–40 K, –5.18%), and October (–18 K, –2.33%), indicating seasonal downturns or reduced campaign spend.

Overall, despite fluctuations, revenue remained stable across the fiscal year.

# Recommendations

1.	Prioritize Accessories & Electronics:
Allocate higher marketing budgets and inventory stock toward these two categories as they account for the majority of total revenue.

3.	Improve Apparel Performance:
Reassess product quality, pricing strategy, and marketing visibility for the apparel category, which currently trails behind other key segments. Additionally, consider expanding the product range within apparel — such as introducing new styles, seasonal collections, or bundled offers — to attract a broader audience and boost overall category performance.
4.	Enhance Online Sales Experience:
Since online slightly outperforms in-store revenue, consider investing in digital ad spend, faster checkout, and loyalty programs to amplify growth.

6.	Monitor Seasonality:
Investigate causes for revenue dips in Q1 and Q4; align campaigns with seasonal peaks (e.g., back-to-school, mid-year sales).

8.	Expand PayPal Promotions:
Leverage PayPal’s popularity with cashback or reward-based incentives to encourage repeat purchases.
